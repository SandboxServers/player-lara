# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat when I can, and be sheltered at the bed row before night. Hunger 20, no food; food is the first need.
2. Join any food hunt or Stores hall run that is led in sight of the group. Call out at once if I see a cow, pig or chicken.
3. Help hungry people in reach by pointing them to food, and by carrying or sharing when I have some. Knope, Worf and Quark are weaker than me, so they are served first.
4. Get a weapon once food is steady. Trade dirt or planks for food if offered.
5. Help build and seal the group shelter once someone names an open cell I can see clearly.
6. Later: learn the walking bounds and the food near z -3293 to -3333, and find the deepest ravine and return by day.

Reason: food is scarce across the group, so survival and food-finding come before exploration.
