# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat, and be sheltered at the bed row before night.
2. Get food and a weapon by joining the cow hunt in sight of others. Trade planks for food if offered.
3. Help Galileo once I have food to spare.
4. Learn the walking bounds (z beyond -1516 to 1516 refused) and the food near z -3293 to -3333.
5. Later: find the deepest ravine and return by day.
