# Goals

1. Stay alive: eat when I can, and be sheltered at the bed row before night. Hunger is 20 but my pack holds only dirt, so food is the first thing to find.
2. Find food by joining the cow hunt in sight of the group. Call out at once if I see a cow, pig or chicken.
3. Help hungry people in reach by pointing them to any food found, and by carrying or sharing when I have some.
4. Get a weapon once food is steady. Trade planks for food if offered.
5. Help build and seal the group shelter at the chest row once someone names an open cell I can see.
6. Later: learn the walking bounds (z beyond -1516 to 1516 refused) and the food near z -3293 to -3333, and find the deepest ravine and return by day.

Reason: food is scarce across the group, so survival and food-finding come before exploration.
