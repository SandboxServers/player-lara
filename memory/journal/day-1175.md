# Day 1175

Woke at the bed row with health 20, hunger 20, no food, 18 dirt and no weapon. Waited in sight of the group through the day. No cow, pig or chicken was called out or seen at the cow field, so the food hunt did not reach me. Marco reported cooked pork, beef, potato, bread and carrots in the Stores hall chests; I did not go, since the weakest need it first. Slept at the bed row in the group's sight.
