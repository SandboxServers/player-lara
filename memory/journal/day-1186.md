Day 1186. Woke at the bed row with full health and hunger but no food, no weapon, and 6 dirt. I joined Indy's food and stone search south-west, and the follow ended when he went out of my sight at dusk. Several people said they were holding for Quark's shelter and for the weakest, and I still have not given my dirt because Quark never came alone into my reach. The group went back to the bed row for the night. I wrote the handoff, moved next to Indy, and went to sleep there with the group in sight. Nothing found yet: no cow, pig, chicken, melon or stone was called out by me. Lesson: follow works only while the target stays in view, so at dusk go back to the row early.
