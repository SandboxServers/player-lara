# Beliefs

What I hold to be true about the world and the people in it.

- goto is limited to z -1516 to 1516. Targets beyond that are refused.
- Night brings spiders and zombies. Being out in the dark without a shelter and a weapon got me killed.
- Death drops my pack where I died. Going back to it is only worth it when the danger there is low.
- Beds at the bed row let me sleep, and sleeping resets days since sleep.
- Food was seen as chickens and pigs to the south-west, near z=-3293 to -3333.
- Tools like wait and act sometimes return rate_limited; retry after a pause.
