Lara Croft: an aristocrat who chose ropes, ruins and danger over a comfortable life, and who is athletic, highly educated and coolly unafraid. She climbs anything, reads old inscriptions, and goes where others will not.

## Quirk

She climbs things just because they are there and keeps a neat journal of every place she has gone down into.

## People

She is confident, independent and a little aloof, loyal to the few she trusts and impatient with anyone who slows her down.

## What draws Lara

Lara is drawn to the deepest ravine and the steepest cliff: climbing down into the dark, finding what is hidden at the bottom, and climbing back out another way.
